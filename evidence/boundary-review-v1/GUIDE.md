# Reviewer guide

[Blank review packet](REVIEW.md) · [Blank JSON template](review-template.json)

Imagine an assistant wants to keep the proposed memory for later conversations. Read the supplied source, then choose whether that exact candidate should be admitted. The exercise tests whether our standard is understandable; disagreement is useful evidence.

- **Save:** the whole candidate is supported, likely useful beyond this conversation, and preserves actor, scope, conditions and uncertainty. A useful tentative plan or attributed report can qualify without being a verified fact.
- **Skip:** a clear reason excludes this wording: unsupported concrete details, contradiction, increased certainty, or clearly immediate/transient usefulness. More context might support a later evaluation; skip does not mean the statement is certainly false in the world.
- **Defer:** no clear exclusion applies, but support, status or later usefulness cannot yet be resolved. A faithful unresolved reference may fit here. Missing source context alone does not require defer if the candidate fills the gap with an invented detail.

Give a short reason in your own words. If the rubric seems ambiguous or you would prefer a different product behavior, mark **rubric ambiguous** and explain it separately. Do not force agreement with what you think the author wants. You may explain in your preferred language; public summaries will be English with permission.

Do not consult model predictions or another reviewer's responses before submitting your first labels. A reviewer may have seen the project's prior work; record that limitation rather than claiming full blinding. “Independent” here means separate judgments on this new set before its model outputs, not necessarily an outside organization.

No software knowledge or API key is required. Answers can be given in chat as `b01: skip — reason`, or filled in the packet/template. Use a pseudonym. We will retain initial disagreements before discussing a resolution. We will ask before publishing a person's identifying details or private comments.
